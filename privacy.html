Privacy

Updated 19 October 2026

Artle is a daily art guessing game. You can play without creating an account. This page explains the information used by the game and its service providers.

Game progress and preferences

Your guesses, scores, game progress, language and settings are saved in your browser using local storage. They are not sent to an Artle player database. Clearing Artle’s site data in your browser removes that saved progress and those preferences.

Hosting and analytics

Artle is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers receive technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests a page or image.

The game uses Google Analytics to measure visits and use of the site. Google may process information such as device and browser details, page interactions, identifiers and IP addresses, and may use cookies. This privacy page does not load analytics or advertising scripts.

Read GitHub’s privacy statement and how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can manage cookies in your browser and use Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on.

Advertising

Advertising is currently disabled. Artle does not currently load the AdSense advertising script or request ads.

We are preparing one small Google AdSense banner below the results after all three rounds are completed. It will only be enabled after the site is approved and a Google-certified consent setup has been published and tested.

When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage or similar identifiers to deliver and measure ads, limit repeated ads and prevent fraud. Depending on your choices and applicable requirements, this may include personalized advertising. Non-personalized advertising can also use cookies or identifiers.

Before activation, we will provide consent choices where required and a way to reopen those choices and withdraw consent. No advertising consent tool is active yet. You can also read Google’s privacy policy and manage your Google advertising preferences in My Ad Center.

External links

Links to museums, Wikimedia, Kviss and other websites lead to services with their own privacy policies.
